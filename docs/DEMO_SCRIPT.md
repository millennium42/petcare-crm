# Commercial demo script

1. Enter as `admin@petcare.local` and show the operational indicators.
2. Open customers and pets to demonstrate the one-to-many relationship.
3. Show vaccine due dates, service history and the next appointment.
4. Add a clinical observation, payment or reminder as staff.
5. Open user management to explain the three roles.
6. Sign out and enter as `cliente@petcare.local`.
7. Show that the customer portal contains only that tutor's pets and history.
8. Close with the LGPD/security baseline and the explicit production hardening checklist.
